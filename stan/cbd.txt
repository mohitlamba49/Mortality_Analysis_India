MODEL: Cairns–Blake–Dowd

DATA:
   N, A, Y
   age[n], year[n]
   age_centered[n]
   deaths[n], exposure[n]

PARAMETERS:
   alpha[A]
   kappa1_raw[Y]
   kappa2_raw[Y]

   sigma_alpha
   sigma_kappa1
   sigma_kappa2
   phi

TRANSFORM:
   kappa1 = center(kappa1_raw)
   kappa2 = center(kappa2_raw)

MODEL:
   Priors:
       alpha ~ Normal(0, sigma_alpha)
       sigma_* ~ Normal priors
       phi ~ Gamma(10,1)

   Random walks:
       kappa1[t] depends on kappa1[t-1]
       kappa2[t] depends on kappa2[t-1]

   Likelihood:
       FOR n:
           log(mx) = alpha[age[n]]
                   + kappa1[year[n]]
                   + age_centered[n] * kappa2[year[n]]

           deaths[n] ~ NegBinomial(log(exposure[n]) + log(mx), phi)

GENERATED:
   log_lik
   y_rep
