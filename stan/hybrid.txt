MODEL: HYBRID (RH + ML)

STEP 1:
   Fit RH model (Bayesian)

STEP 2:
   Compute residuals:
       residual = log(mx_observed) - log(mx_RH)

STEP 3:
   Train ML model (XGBoost):
       residual ~ features(age, year, cohort, lag, interactions)

STEP 4:
   Final model:
       log(mx_final) = log(mx_RH) + residual_pred

NOTE:
   - RH captures structure
   - XGBoost captures nonlinear deviations
