MODEL: Age–Period–Cohort

DATA:
   N, A, Y, C
   age[n], year[n], cohort[n]
   deaths[n], exposure[n]

PARAMETERS:
   alpha[A]
   kappa_std[Y]
   gamma_std[C]

   sigma_alpha
   sigma_kappa
   sigma_gamma
   phi

TRANSFORM:
   Construct kappa via cumulative sum
   Construct gamma via cumulative sum

   Center both:
       kappa = kappa - mean(kappa)
       gamma = gamma - mean(gamma)

   Remove linear trends:
       detrend kappa and gamma

MODEL:
   Priors:
       alpha ~ Normal(0, sigma_alpha)
       kappa_std ~ Normal(0,1)
       gamma_std ~ Normal(0,1)
       sigma_* ~ Normal(0, small variance)
       phi ~ Gamma(10,1)

   Likelihood:
       FOR n:
           log(mx) = alpha[age[n]] + kappa[year[n]] + gamma[cohort[n]]
           deaths[n] ~ NegBinomial(log(exposure[n]) + log(mx), phi)

GENERATED:
   log_lik
   y_rep
