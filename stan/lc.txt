MODEL: Lee–Carter

DATA:
   N, A, Y
   age[n], year[n]
   deaths[n], exposure[n]

PARAMETERS:
   alpha[A]         # age effect
   beta[A]          # age sensitivity (sum to 1)
   kappa_raw[Y]     # time index

   sigma_alpha
   sigma_kappa
   phi

TRANSFORM:
   kappa = center(kappa_raw)

MODEL:
   Priors:
       alpha ~ Normal(0, sigma_alpha)
       beta ~ Dirichlet(1,...,1)
       sigma_alpha ~ Normal(0,2)
       sigma_kappa ~ Normal(0,2)
       phi ~ Gamma(10,1)

   Random walk:
       kappa[1] ~ Normal(0,1)
       FOR t=2..Y:
           kappa[t] ~ Normal(kappa[t-1], sigma_kappa)

   Likelihood:
       FOR n:
           log(mx) = alpha[age[n]] + beta[age[n]] * kappa[year[n]]
           deaths[n] ~ NegBinomial(log(exposure[n]) + log(mx), phi)

GENERATED:
   compute log_lik
   simulate y_rep
